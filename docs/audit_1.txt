FEMPRENEUR 2027

WEBSITE AUDIT & CONTENT GAP ANALYSIS

Prepared by cross-referencing the Fempreneur 2027 Partnership Proposal, the Fempreneur Website Architecture Report (05 June 2026), and the live Greenpreneur.in build — using the same audit framework used before Greenpreneur.in was built.

September 2026

Unlike the Greenpreneur audit — which had to reconstruct the brand's identity from external research because no proposal document existed yet — this audit starts from richer source material: a full 2027 partnership proposal (mission, pillars, award mechanics, sponsorship tiers, city strategy) and a prior website-architecture report that already defines the menu and page priorities. This document does three things: (1) confirms what's already strong enough to publish as-is, (2) maps what's missing against the same categories used in the Greenpreneur audit (pages, features, content, technical, opportunities), and (3) flags a handful of inconsistencies across the source documents that should be resolved before the site goes live.

Credibility source — keep organiser credits visible in the footer on every page, as Greenpreneur does.

2027 edition runs in two cities — Ahmedabad and Delhi NCR — expanded from Ahmedabad-only in 2022–2025.

This is a structural change, not a footnote — the site needs to support two distinct city journeys, not one.

Unique Mechanism

50% jury evaluation + 50% public voting, with each nominee receiving a personal, shareable voting link.

This is Fempreneur's single most differentiating — and most technically demanding — feature. No competitor page in the architecture report currently plans for it.

Track Record

6+ editions since 2022, 500+ entrepreneurs per event, 35–40+ categories, 10,000+ stories published, 60+ expert speakers, 5L+ digital reach per edition.

Strong proof points — surface as an animated stats bar on the homepage, the way Greenpreneur's wireframe opens with its impact numbers.

Data-consistency flags — resolve before build

•  Category count is inconsistent across the proposal: the summary stat says "35+ Award Categories," the Connect pillar says "150+ Business and Industry Categories," and the printed list runs to 40 named categories. Confirm one number before it goes on the site.

•  Two domains appear in the source documents — Fempreneur.in (used throughout the proposal and the architecture report) and Fempreneur.club (used on the proposal's closing page). Confirm the canonical domain before launch.

•  The architecture report's Tier 3 list includes a "Why Gandhinagar" page, while the Event Hub menu and the proposal both use "Why Ahmedabad" / "Why Ahmedabad & Delhi NCR." This looks like a leftover from another project — recommend renaming it to match the proposal's actual two-city rationale (Section 11).

The proposal document has already done the writing for several pages — this is content the site can use almost verbatim rather than starting from a blank page.

2. Sponsorship structure — Six full tiers with itemised benefits — Title (₹5,00,000), Powered By (₹3,00,000), Platinum (₹1,50,000), Gold (₹1,00,000), Silver (₹50,000), Category Sponsor (₹10,000) — ready for a Sponsorship Opportunities page and a tier-comparison table.

3. Eight participation pathways — Nominate · Attend · Sponsor · Feature in the Book · Get a Story on VyapaarJagat · Join the Community · Exhibit · Speak — each already has a paragraph of copy in Section 8, and maps directly to eight distinct landing pages or CTA blocks.

4. 35–40 award categories — Already named individually in Section 6 — ready for the Award Categories page once the category-count inconsistency above is resolved.

5. SDG + Viksit Bharat @2047 alignment — A genuinely unique differentiator (SDG 5, 8, 9, 10, 17 plus a six-point Viksit Bharat narrative) that no competitor referenced in the architecture report has — ready to become its own page or homepage section.

6. Why Ahmedabad & Delhi NCR — A seven-point side-by-side comparison table already exists in Section 11 — ready-made content for the "Why These Two Cities" page once retitled correctly (see flag above).

7. Full event-day program — Nine named elements — Nominations, Felicitation, Book Launch, Keynotes & Panels, Exhibition Showcase, Masterclasses, Fireside Chats, Open Networking, Story Drive — ready for the Event Agenda page.

3.2 Features to Develop

Feature

Description

Why It Matters

Nomination + Public Voting System

Online nomination form feeding a jury-scoring backend, plus a public voting portal with a personalised, shareable link per nominee and a live or gated vote tally.

This is Fempreneur's core mechanic and its biggest technical build — nothing comparable exists on Greenpreneur, which is jury-only.

Vote Integrity Controls

One vote per verified user (OTP or social login), rate limiting, and bot/fraud detection on the voting portal.

Public voting is only credible if it can't be gamed — this protects the 50% public-vote result.

"Get Pass" Delegate Registration

Event ticketing with a city selector (Ahmedabad vs Delhi NCR), capacity tracking, and payment gateway.

Currently just a sticky CTA button in the architecture report with no backend defined.

Sponsor / Partner Inquiry Flow

Tier-selection form that auto-generates a proposal PDF or routes straight to the sales team.

Turns the six-tier sponsorship table into a working lead-capture tool instead of a static page.

Speaker & Masterclass Application

Application form for experts wanting to speak or lead a masterclass, per pathway 08 in the proposal.

Currently only described in prose — needs an actual form.

Exhibitor Application

Form for the 50 curated women-led businesses in the Exhibition Showcase, per pathway 07.

Slots are described as limited — a form with a capacity counter creates urgency.

Coffee Table Book Ordering

E-commerce page to pre-order/purchase the book, with page previews and delivery info — mirrors Greenpreneur's book-ordering page.

Converts a proof-point ("5,000+ print copies") into a revenue line and a shareable object.

Story Submission Portal

Intake form feeding the VyapaarJagat 1,000-story drive, cross-linked to VyapaarJagat.com.

Currently the story drive is described but has no submission mechanism on either site.

Winner Profile Pages

Individual, filterable pages per winner across 2022–2025, by year, category and city.

Fempreneur has 4+ prior editions of winners with no directory yet — high-value, low-effort SEO content.

Membership / Community Portal

Sign-up flow for the permanent Fempreneur community described in pathway 06, with any city-circle structure once defined.

Converts a one-day event audience into a year-round list.

3.4 Technical Improvements Needed

Issue

Recommendation

Mobile responsiveness

Non-negotiable — India is mobile-first, and the voting portal specifically will see most of its traffic from nominees sharing links on WhatsApp.

Fast loading during voting windows

Optimise images and use a CDN; public-voting periods will create sharp traffic spikes as nominees push their links.

SEO optimisation

Target "women entrepreneur awards India," "women entrepreneurship platform India," "Fempreneur Awards 2027," plus city-modified variants for Ahmedabad and Delhi NCR.

Accessibility

Alt text, proper contrast, keyboard navigation — especially on the voting and nomination forms, which need to be usable by the widest possible public audience.

SSL certificate

Confirm HTTPS is properly configured, particularly given the site will process payments (passes, book orders) and personal data (nominations, votes).

Multi-language support

Hindi is worth prioritising given the national, two-city 2027 scope; Gujarati remains relevant for the Ahmedabad base.

Voting-system security & scalability

Specific to Fempreneur — the public-vote mechanic needs its own load-testing and abuse-prevention plan, not just general site hardening.

4. Structural Principles Worth Stealing

Carried over from the architecture report — these hold regardless of which pages get built first:

From EntrepreneurIndia — Separate sub-journeys by visitor intent. Someone who wants to attend should never land on the same page as someone who wants to sponsor — build intent-based navigation, and route "Get Pass" and "Become a Partner" down genuinely different paths.

From IBDA — Jury credibility is a trust multiplier. A dedicated, photo-led Jury Panel page matters more than its position in the nav suggests — people nominate when they respect the judges, and right now Fempreneur has zero juror content.

From BW Merit Awards — A "Why Participate" bullet section converts. Add it to the Award Overview, Sponsorship, and Speaker/Exhibitor pages — not just once.

From Greenpreneur.in (sibling brand, same organisers) — Reuse the homepage rhythm that's already proven for 1MEIF properties — stat bar near the top, program spotlight, winner spotlight, flagship-deliverable showcase, impact metrics, community CTA, newsletter — rather than inventing a new pattern for Fempreneur.

6. Summary — Priority Order

The site's biggest gap isn't content — most of the persuasive copy already exists in the proposal. The gap is (a) the five Tier 1 pages that still need fresh content (Jury Panel, Why Nominate condensed, FAQ, Speakers, Gallery), and (b) the nomination + public-voting system, which is the one feature with no equivalent on Greenpreneur.in and no head start in either source document.

Immediate priorities

•  Resolve the three data-consistency flags (category count, domain, Why Gandhinagar vs Why Ahmedabad/Delhi NCR) before writing final copy.

•  Build the five Tier 1 pages before 25 June — Event Page, Award Overview, Award Categories, Jury Panel, Why Nominate.

•  Scope the nomination + public-voting system early — it is the longest technical lead time of anything on this list and the feature most unique to Fempreneur.

•  Port the already-written proposal content (sponsorship tiers, SDG/Viksit Bharat alignment, two-city rationale, story drive) directly into pages rather than rewriting it from scratch.
