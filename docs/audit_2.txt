Nirav Saraiya, a 35-year-old engineer from Ahmedabad, 

transformed his community's water crisis through innovative 

conservation technology.

THE PROBLEM

Ahmedabad faces severe water scarcity. During summer, many 

neighborhoods receive water only 2-3 days per week. Nirav's 

community of 500 households faced 15-day water shortages.

THE SOLUTION

Nirav developed a rainwater harvesting system combined with 

wastewater recycling:

• 500-liter capacity rainwater collectors on 100 rooftops

• Gravity-fed filtration system (no electricity needed)

• Wastewater treatment plant recycling 50,000 liters/day

• Smart monitoring system tracking water levels

THE IMPACT

• Water Saved: 1,000,000 liters/year

• Communities Served: 500 households

• Cost Reduction: ₹200,000/year in water bills

• CO₂ Reduced: 50 tons/year (less electricity for pumping)

RECOGNITION

Nirav won the Fempreneur 2025 Award in Water Category. 

His project was featured in the Fempreneur Coffee Table 

Book 2025.

FUTURE PLANS

Nirav is scaling his system to 10 more neighborhoods in 

Ahmedabad. He's also partnering with Gujarat Government 

to implement this across 50 villages.

"Purpose over profit is the key. When you solve real 

problems, money follows." – Nirav Saraiya
